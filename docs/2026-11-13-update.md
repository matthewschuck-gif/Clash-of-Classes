## Pulse (06_Polls.gs)
- New Kind `Pulse`:
  - no winner, a participation point per voter
  - results show live right after a vote
  - open Date through Through (new column)
- Votes record Grade, and Results get a by-grade breakdown. Votes are dated the day they're cast.
- `clashPulseLive()` feeds the new **Pulse** page and TV slide 9: Menti-style bars plus a squad
  turnout race. Counts only.
- **Poll Ideas** tab: 40 starter questions, each with why the school wants to know and who uses it.
- Menu › **Add the next Pulse question** (Settings › `PULSE_DAYS`, default 3).

## Freeze and thaw (07_Freezes.gs, 14_Thaw.gs)
- New thaw goal `votes: N`: poll and Pulse votes since the freeze began.
- Settings › `FRESH_START` (menu › **Fresh start: end every freeze on a date…**):
  - every freeze that began before the date ends the day before
  - a Fun Freeze cashes out its tier
  - the fresh-start day's points count even before the check runs
